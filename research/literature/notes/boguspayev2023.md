# Selected-source check: Boguspayev et al. (2023)

Source ID: `boguspayev2023`. DOI: [10.3390/app13084819](https://doi.org/10.3390/app13084819). Checked 2026-11-01 using Crossref metadata and supplied PDF pages 2-3 and 5-7. Publisher browsing returned rate limits.

The examined passages distinguish position/velocity inputs in loose coupling from raw-observation interfaces in tight coupling. They support the integration background used in R03. Deep coupling is introduced, but page 6 excludes it from detailed treatment; avoid implying equally detailed coverage of all three architectures.

Selecting a loosely coupled error-state EKF is our design proposal, not a comparative result established by this survey. Performance and sensor parameters were not independently reviewed. Full review remains pending.
