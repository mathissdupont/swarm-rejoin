## Method, evaluation quality and limits

The paper's question concerns a physical/virtual sensor-emulation test framework rather than a new secure updater. Section III combines motion capture, simulated sensors and PX4/ROS 2 interfaces. Section V demonstrates navigation-measurement corruption and external-measurement mitigation on one actual vehicle. This is sensor-data emulation, not evidence that this repository has conducted an over-the-air GNSS attack or any physical flight.

Section V / PDF page 4 describes a two-minute latency recording and a separately described attack/mitigation demonstration. A latency recording's samples are not equivalent to independent mission repetitions; an independently replicated statistical mission comparison is not supplied by our reading. Do not import the paper's delay numbers or hardware performance into our abstract simulator.

Equation 1 / page 4 decomposes total emulation delay into motion-capture, simulation and transport components. The proposed additional constant delay is conditional on the emulation pipeline being faster than the target sensor, as its footnote states. Adding a constant adjusts location of a delay distribution; it does not alone establish matching variance, correlation or tail behavior. The empirical tuning demonstration must not be promoted to a universal fidelity guarantee.

Motion capture, external cameras and the stated compute platform are material setup assumptions. Our first computer-only model can borrow the separation of truth, observations and transport delay, but cannot claim to reproduce that physical setup. Section VI explicitly leaves mixed real/virtual swarm experiments to future work. The existence of virtual-vehicle containers in the architecture is not proof of demonstrated fleet-level resilience.

## Relevance and next acceptance step

Use this as a deep test-fidelity/benchmark reference alongside the RASUES update-state review. The candidate question is whether combined update/recovery and navigation-evidence cases add useful characterization under explicitly lower-fidelity assumptions. It is refuted as a contribution if existing artifacts already cover the same contract or our synthetic model cannot support the inference.

The paper links an open-source implementation, but we did not install it, verify the current repository state or confirm complete reproduction artifacts. Another author should review our distinction between physical demonstration, architecture capability and future swarm work before it supports a final novelty statement.
